1 IntroductionThe subject for our innovation work is to test antimicrobial effects on surfaces, after abrasion or mechanical wear. The abrasion done artificially with equipment called BRAIVE Instrument Washability and the materials are provided by the customer, ISKU. Tested materials are from ISKU+ collection, and the antimicrobial effects are based on ionised silver coating. Materials may be various, specific ones have not been confirmed yet, but can be textile, laminate or lacquer coated wooden surfaces. Most likely laminate.Testing is going to be based on international standard ISO 22196, Plastics — Measurement of antibacterial activity on plastics surfaces. The standard is referring to plastic material with anti-bacterial treatment, not for example, plastic building materials [1, s.1.]. That said, there is no universal standard for testing antimicrobic surfaces in general, so standard mentioned before is applied for this project.2 Apparatuses and materials* BRAIVE Instruments Washability * antimicrobial materials from TEKNOS to test the BRAIVE instrument* materials from ISKU+ collection, e.g., textile, laminate, lacquer coated wooden surfaces* biological materials for culture mediums (ISO 22196:2007) * apparatus and materials listed in ISO 22196:2007
3 Risks evaluation To prevent infection with E. coli and S. aureus, gloves should be worn, and hands should be washed before leaving the lab. The tables should always be wiped before and after working in the lab with 70 % ethanol. The risk of contamination during pipetting is avoided by always keeping one pipette close to one substance to be pipetted. Replace the pipette head if you are not sure it is clean.
